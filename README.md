# Pog Guild Tools

A guild home base for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Type **/pog** to open it. It looks like part of the game: the game's own window frame, gold lettering, buttons and sounds, in a choice of themes.

Everything your guild needs in one window: a message board and group finder, profiles, raid rosters, attendance, a loot log, a crafting directory, raid plans, and a few games to play while you wait for the summon.

## Install

- **CurseForge:** [Pog Guild Tools](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/pog-guild-tools), or search "Pog Guild Tools" in the CurseForge app.
- **WowUp:** search "Pog Guild Tools".
- **By hand:** download the zip from CurseForge and put the `PogRaid` folder inside it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`.

The shared features (message board, group finder, profiles, crafting, leaderboards, duels, raid plans, attendance) work between guildies who run Pog Guild Tools, so get your guild on it together.

## Home

Your guild at a glance: the guild's name and message of the day, who's online (Show offline lists everyone else too, with their level), level-ups and achievements, guild chat, and the guild's recent blue-and-better drops.

- **Message board.** Officers post announcements and can pin them; everyone with Pog Guild Tools can comment.
- **Group finder.** Post what you're doing and who you need. Listings show how full the group is (2/5) and expire after 45 minutes. Guildies ask to join with one click and you get a pop-up to invite them. When someone posts a group, a small pop-up lets you ask to join right away.

## Group chat

A small chat window of its own, like an instant messenger. Click New chat, pick one guildie or several from who's online (or type a name to find them) and start a chat: lines go privately between the people in it, a pop-up and a sound tell you about a new one, and your chats are still there next time you log in. Open it with the Chat button on Home, from a guildie's right-click menu, or with `/pog chat`. Whispers to and from guildmates are kept in your chat with them too, and a line to a guildie without the addon goes out as an ordinary whisper, so it works with the whole guild. Group chats of three or more need everyone on Pog Guild Tools 0.16.5 or newer. A group chat can be given its own name and picture, shared with everyone in it. Your latest chats and what's unread show on the Home page. Settings turns chat off; the ticks at the top of the chat window mute its sound, stop its pop-ups, write new lines to your game chat box, or keep whispers out of it.

## Profiles

A page for every guildie running Pog Guild Tools, one per account.

- **Make it yours.** A nickname, a status line, About Me, favorites, main role, a portrait from thousands of classic icons, a theme color and a profile song that plays when guildies visit. Set it once and all your characters share it.
- **Kudos.** Recognize guildies for Clutch heals, Great tank, Carried me, GGWP and more, with a few words saying why. They get a pop-up when you do.
- **Professions.** Their two main professions, with their recipes a click away.
- **Character.** Their gear laid out like the character pane, with their stats (or their character in 3D when they're nearby), and Inspect to see what they were most recently wearing, even when they're offline. Click an item to add it to your own wishlist.
- **Most played with.** Fills itself with the guildies you group with most.

## LFG

- **Find a group.** Tick the dungeons you want, pick your roles (tank, healer, DPS, any mix), add a note, and the guild sees you're looking for the next four hours. Dungeons in your level range stand out.
- **Groups at a glance.** Every group listing as a tile with how full it is and what it needs, and a Join button right on it. People after the same dungeons as you are listed first.

## Wishlist

- **Plan your gear.** A character sheet of the gear you're after, one item per slot, with your character in the middle wearing it. Shift-click items in from your bags, chat or any loot window. Keep up to three wishlists and name them.
- **Browse by slot.** Pick any slot to see every item for it, best item level first, and where it drops. Items tick themselves off when you get them.

## Raid

- **Roster.** Build raid rosters from your guild with drag and drop: 10, 20 or 40 players, a bench, tanks and healers marked by hand, available / tentative / out, composition counts, one-click invites and automatic group arranging. Copy the roster for Discord.
- **Attendance.** Records itself while an officer, the raid leader or an assistant is in the raid, and counts by the week: raid with either team and the week counts. Arriving late or leaving early without a word counts half, measured from when the raid actually gathered and broke up. Say "I'll be late" or "I'll leave early" with one click (or `/pog late`, `/pog early`) and you keep full credit. Officers' raid nights are shared as they happen, so both teams add up and a crash doesn't lose the night.
- **Loot log.** Who got what, from which boss, on which night, with main spec / off spec tags. CSV and Discord export.
- **Raid Plans.** Draw on the raid's own map, or sketch a room from scratch, then place raid markers, players, role groups, arrows and labels. Press "Show to raid" and it pops up on everyone's screen, with their own spot highlighted.

## Crafting

- **Who can make it?** Search any recipe and see which guildies know it, online crafters first, with a click to whisper them. Works for crafters who are offline too.
- **Profession directory.** Every profession in the guild, who has it and their skill.
- Your professions share themselves. Open each of your profession windows once and your recipes join the search.

## Games

- **Wowdle.** A daily WoW word puzzle, the same word for the whole guild, with everyone's results for the day.
- **Sapper Sweeper.** Minesweeper with Goblin Sapper Charges, in Apprentice, Journeyman and Expert, with a guild leaderboard.
- **Gem Cutter.** Slide and merge gems up to a Large Opal, with a guild best-score board.
- **Ranked Duels.** Challenge a guildie, duel, and the result counts once both addons agree. Chess-style ratings and a win/loss leaderboard.

## Settings

- **Themes:** Night, Stone, Ember, Sandstone or Leather, each on its own textured surface, or the plain modern look.
- The minimap button (easy to hide), sounds, pop-ups, profile songs, which guild ranks count as officers, and who in the guild has Pog Guild Tools and on which version.

## Commands

- `/pog` opens and closes the window.
- `/pog home`, `/pog roster`, `/pog attendance`, `/pog loot`, `/pog plans`, `/pog crafting`, `/pog games`, `/pog settings` open a tab. `/pog wowdle`, `/pog gems` and `/pog duels` open a game. `/pog chat` opens group chat.
- `/pog profile` opens your profile; `/pog profile Name` opens someone else's.
- `/pog late`, `/pog early` and `/pog ontime` tell the raid leaders about tonight.
- `/pog minimap` shows or hides the minimap button. `/pog reset` puts the windows back in the middle of the screen.
- `/pog bug` lists any Pog Guild Tools errors in a box you can copy, ready to send to the author.

## For officers

- Officers are the guild master and the rank below by default. Change which ranks count in **Settings > Officer ranks**; it's shared with the whole guild.
- Attendance starts by itself when you lead, assist or are an officer inside a raid instance. Start raid / End raid on the Attendance tab work by hand too.
- Pog Guild Tools tells you in chat when a newer version is out. Keep the guild on the same version where you can.

## Good to know

- Everything is stored in the game's own saved data, per account. No outside services or websites.
- Built for big guilds: shared data catches up in small pieces within the game's message limits, and raid plans always go out first.
