# Pog Guild Tools 0.16.9

- **New game: Motherlode.** Bomb your way down a mine on the Games tab (or /pog motherlode). Aim with the mouse and click to drop a bomb: every peg it bounces off is mined, and the pocket it lands in multiplies the haul. Reach each floor's ore quota before you run out of bombs, then pick one of three upgrades and go deeper. Bombs glow and trail sparks, ore numbers float off what you mine, kegs go up in a ring, and a click still counts if your aim takes the mouse off the board. Your deepest run goes on the guild hiscores, which show on the board before and after a run (or any time from the Hiscores button).
- **Level-up pop-ups.** When a guildie reaches level 20, 30, 40 or 50 you get a pop-up, and reaching 60 gets a banner across the top of your screen. They don't need the addon for you to see it. Settings turns them off; /pog ding shows you what the level 60 one looks like.
- **Open Games on flight paths.** A new Settings tick (off unless you turn it on): take a flight path and the Games tab opens by itself.
- **Fixed: profile songs playing with the window closed.** A guildie's profile song could start by itself after you closed Pog Guild Tools. Pages no longer redraw in the background while the window is closed.
- **Click to reply.** With "In game chat" ticked, the name in brackets at the start of a chat line in your game chat box is now a link. Click it to open that chat, ready to type.

New in 0.16.8:

- **Chats on Home.** A new Chats card under the Message board shows your latest chats, their last line and how many lines are unread. Click one to open it, or New chat to start one. It replaces the small Chat button next to Show offline.
- **Name your group chats.** A group chat can have its own name and picture, shared with everyone in it. Open the chat and click Edit, or right-click it in the list.
- **Chat switches.** New ticks at the top of the chat window: Sound mutes the new-line sound, Pop-ups stops the pop-up, and In game chat also writes new lines to your game chat box (off unless you tick it).
- **Pictures in the chat list.** Each chat shows the other person's portrait, or the group's picture.

New in 0.16.7:

- **Chat now works with the whole guild.** Whispers to and from guildmates are kept in your chat with them, so the chat window is your whisper history too. A line to someone without the addon (marked "whisper") goes out as an ordinary whisper, and item links in whispers can be clicked. Group chats of three or more still need everyone on Pog Guild Tools. Untick "Guild whispers" at the top of the chat window to keep whispers out of it.
- **New chat button.** The chat window's list is now just the people you've messaged. Click New chat to pick who to message: search by name, click one person or several for a group, then Start chat.
- **Fixed in 0.16.6: the Chat button on Home seemed to do nothing.** The chat window was opening behind the main window.

New in 0.16.5:

- **New: group chat.** A small chat window of its own, like an instant messenger: pick one guildie or several from who's online and start a chat. Lines go privately between the people in it, a pop-up and a sound tell you about a new one, and your chats are kept between sessions. Open it with the Chat button on Home (it counts unread lines), from a guildie's right-click menu, or with /pog chat. Everyone in a chat needs this version or newer. Turn it off in Settings if it's not for you.
- **New: kudos pop-up.** When a guildie gives you kudos, a pop-up says who, what for and their message. Click it to open your profile. Settings turns it off.
- **Recent loot is readable again.** Farming a dungeon no longer fills the card: each player's item shows once, and one player takes at most 8 lines. Your own repeat drops are shared once, not every time.
- **Fairer attendance.** Anyone in the raid by the end of the first boss attempt is on time, however long the raid took to form.
- **Wowdle no longer takes your keyboard** when you open Games. Click the board to type, or use /pog wowdle.
- **Ranked duels:** "Challenge your target" now reaches them (it was whispering the first name only).
- **Roster:** Invite roster and Arrange groups recognise raid members by first name or full name. Pugs you raid with no longer join the raider pool; players you add by hand still do.
- **Smoother:** less background chatter between addons when group listings expire, faster name lookups, and the Inspect window no longer redraws on every item that loads.
- **Looks:** the Wishlist has two even columns of armour with the weapons in a row along the bottom, like the character pane. Activity no longer says "now" for level-ups that happened while you were away. "Most played with" shows first names that fit. Wowdle's stats bars match the theme.
- **LFG:** the Cozy Sleeping Bag quest chain is no longer listed as a dungeon.
